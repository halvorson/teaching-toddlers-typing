### WR-04: `sharePending` re-entrancy flag isn't scoped to a mount, so a Share click can silently no-op after a quick screen change

**Files modified:** `src/menu.ts`
**Commit:** `b67e847`
**Applied fix:** Reset `sharePending = false` at the top of both `mountMenu()` and `unmountMenu()`, mirroring the existing `mountGeneration++` reset that already runs at both boundaries. This closes the gap where a `runShare()` call left in flight across an unmount/remount (e.g. Share click → quick Settings → Back) held `sharePending = true` for the newly mounted menu, silently no-op'ing the very next Share click until the stale promise settled. The stale call's own DOM-mutating side effects were already correctly discarded via the pre-existing `myGeneration !== mountGeneration` check in `runShare()`'s `finally`-guarded body — this fix only addresses the flag itself blocking new, legitimate clicks.
