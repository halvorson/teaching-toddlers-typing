## Fixed Issues

### WR-05: `mountMenu()` isn't defensively idempotent, unlike `mountGameScreen()` and `mountSettingsScreen()`

**Files modified:** `src/menu.ts`
**Commit:** b06d970
**Applied fix:** Added `unmountMenu()` as the first statement in `mountMenu()`, matching the pattern already used by `mountGameScreen()` (`game-screen.ts`) and `mountSettingsScreen()` (`settings.ts`). `unmountMenu()` already increments `mountGeneration`, resets `sharePending`, clears listeners, removes the previous `.home-menu` wrapper, and cancels any pending `copiedTimeoutId` — so the redundant standalone `mountGeneration++` / `sharePending = false` lines that previously sat at the top of `mountMenu()` were removed in favor of letting `unmountMenu()` own that reset. This closes the latent gap where a hypothetical back-to-back `mountMenu()` call (not reachable today via `main.ts`'s routing, but previously unguarded) could leave a stale `copiedTimeoutId`/`shareFallbackEl` from the prior mount alive, causing a stale Share-feedback revert timer to fire against the new mount's Share label. Verified with `tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json` (no errors) after the change.
