## Decisions Made
- Appended the trail layer to `document.body` rather than `#app`, since `#app`'s children are wiped by `replaceChildren()` on every screen transition — this keeps the trail's own lifecycle independent of the screen-router's DOM churn.
- Placed the `.star-trail__star` reduced-motion override as its own trailing `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block at the end of `style.css`, following the same wrapper shape as the existing `#target.correct-pulse` reduced-motion block rather than merging into it — kept the two concerns (target pulse vs. star pop-in) visually separate in the stylesheet while reusing the identical pattern.
- `clearTrail()` is fully implemented and exported per the plan's spec even though nothing in this plan calls it yet — plan `02.1-03` (the Settings toggle-gated wipe) is its consumer.
